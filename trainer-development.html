Personal Trainer Mentorship

Simple systems. Tailored feedback. Master your craft.

Mentorship for serious trainers who love training and want structured support, accountability, and real-world feedback to master coaching and build a sustainable business.

My story

Twenty years and 20,000+ sessions later

Those first months nearly broke me. Every client interaction felt like a test I wasn't prepared for. Every assessment was a guessing game. Every program I wrote felt like throwing darts in the dark, hoping something would stick. The imposter syndrome was suffocating.

But here's what I discovered: every master trainer has felt exactly this way. The difference isn't talent, it's having the right guide at the right time. I found mentors who transformed my anxiety into expertise. Twenty years and 20,000+ sessions later, I've made it my mission to be that guide for trainers who are exactly where I was: talented, driven, but desperately needing someone to show them the way.

How I can help

Four areas mentees work on most, depending on where you are in your career.

NASM prep & study guidance

Master the material and pass your certification with confidence.

Client assessments & program troubleshooting

Learn to identify movement issues and create programs that actually work.

Exercise technique & coaching skills

Develop the cueing and correction skills that separate pros from amateurs.

Career strategy & business development

Build a thriving practice that attracts quality clients and premium rates.

Here's how it works

  1. 1

    Discovery session

    We schedule your session and you complete a detailed questionnaire so I can learn about you, your business, and your specific goals before we meet.

  2. 2

    Mentorship

    We meet for one hour, in person or virtual, to discuss your goals, create a personalized roadmap for success, and work on the specific skills, knowledge, and resources you need to achieve them.

  3. 3

    Follow-up & refine

    I follow up with a 15-minute phone call to see how well your plan is working and help you make any necessary adjustments to ensure you're on track for success.
