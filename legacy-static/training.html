What to expect

Experience a transformative fitness journey with tailored personal training solutions. Unlock your potential with expert guidance and dedicated support. Start your path to success today.

Assess your starting point

We begin with a sit-down discussion

I ask you a lot of questions about your goals, training history, injury and health history, and lifestyle. Then we assess your movement quality to identify imbalances and inspire a safe and effective program.

Your program

Customized, and built to make you independent

I build a customized program and work with you to develop your comfort and confidence with it. Though some clients prefer to strictly train with me, I'm going to try to make you as functionally independent as possible. I never ask for long commitments. I need to keep earning your business every step of the way while creating a valuable experience and relationship you want to return to.

Programs

Four ways clients work with Mark, from one-on-one correction to mentoring the next generation of trainers.

Functional movement

For clients dealing with pain, past injury, or movement that just feels off. Sessions rebuild the fundamental patterns: squat, hinge, push, pull, and carry, so your body handles daily life and training without compensating around a weak link.

Strength & conditioning

For clients ready to build capacity, not just correct it. Programming is structured and progressive, adjusted session to session as you get stronger.

Weight management

Training and habit coaching aimed at change you can actually keep, built around your schedule and history rather than a generic calorie target.

Trainer mentorship & workshops

One-on-one mentoring and workshops for personal trainers who want to sharpen their assessment and coaching skills, drawing on Mark's work certifying over 1,000 trainers through NASM.
