What to expect

A process, not a workout playlist

Assess your starting point

We begin with a conversation, then a movement assessment

We start with a sit-down discussion about your goals, training history, injury history, and lifestyle. Then we assess your movement quality to identify imbalances and design a program that's actually safe and effective for your body, not a generic template.

Your program

Built around you, adjusted as you go

I build a customized program and work with you to develop comfort and confidence with it. Some clients prefer to train with me indefinitely; others want to become functionally independent. Either way, I never ask for long commitments. I have to keep earning your business every step of the way.

Programs

Four ways clients work with Mark, from one-on-one correction to mentoring the next generation of trainers.

Functional movement

Rebuild the fundamental patterns: squat, hinge, push, pull, and carry, so you move without compensation or pain.

Strength & conditioning

Structured programming that builds real strength and capacity, adjusted as you progress rather than left on autopilot.

Weight management

Training and habit coaching built around sustainable change, not a six-week sprint you can't maintain.

Trainer mentorship & workshops

One-on-one mentoring and workshops for personal trainers, drawing on Mark's work certifying over 1,000 trainers through NASM.
